# Cognous Engineering Orchestration

Static execution practice under the [Engineering Constitution](ENGINEERING_CONSTITUTION.md), [foundational principles](PRINCIPLES.md) and [adoption rules](ADOPTION.md). Coordinate already authorized work from contract readiness through independent qualification and verified integration. Reduce avoidable waiting, rework and operator interruptions without weakening authority, security, source integrity or independence. This document does not start a worker, configure a scheduler or authorize new work.

## Roles and assignment discipline

The **orchestrator** coordinates authorized assignments, priorities, dependencies, authority records, capacity, review routing, completion gates, eligible integration and consolidated reporting. Its coordination does not create architectural authority, adoption standing or independent verdicts. The acts of proposing, inspecting, certifying and integrating remain separately attributable.

The **implementation owner** owns contract reconciliation, implementation, developer regressions and exact-candidate handoff. The **independent qualification owner** owns specifications, fixtures, adapters, collectors, oracles, evidence and bounded qualification. Neither role gains authority over the other's scope merely through coordination. Independent protocol approval, exact-subject freeze, execution, review and verdict must be attributable; disclose missing independence rather than self-certifying.

Every assignment is self-contained:

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| Identity | Task, work package, role, repository and exact base/source identity. |
| Authority | Authorized objective, controlling decision and resource/spending limits. |
| Ownership | Allowed files/components, designated writer and prohibited changes. |
| Inputs | Frozen contracts, interfaces, profile/configuration and dependencies. |
| Acceptance | Behavior, negative/adversarial controls, applicable independent battery and risk tier. |
| Delivery | Branch/PR where applicable, exact final head, raw evidence and bounded handoff. |
| Boundaries | Stop conditions, exclusions, downstream dependencies and next action. |
| Governance | EP01–EP23, DOC-01 and applicable repository entry instructions. |

Explicitly emphasize EP02's minimum sufficient implementation, EP04's independent expectations before acceptance, EP08's independently qualified outcome, EP09's safe parallelism and EP11's proactive blocker management. Where tools do not discover repository instructions automatically, include them in the assignment. Mutable status belongs in designated telemetry; preserve immutable history separately and run existing required document validation for governed changes.

Before implementation, inspect reusable components, accessible exact sources, contract obligations, ownership, environment, independent oracle readiness and execution authority. Missing essential prerequisites become named blockers; continue independently ready authorized work.

Within scope, finish implement, test, debug, retest, applicable review, reviewable delivery and handoff without routine continuation requests. Keep branches and primary deliverables bounded; separate unrelated defects. Stop at completion, a real blocker or a material authority boundary. Use targeted checks during iteration and required independent qualification at its gate; avoid identical full reruns without changed inputs or a protocol need.

## Resume and supervisory procedure

1. Read repository instructions, the constitution, affected contracts and latest operator decisions. Recover only relevant context.
2. Inspect live telemetry, exact PR heads/diffs/reviews/CI and retained evidence. Verify historical claims; chat alone proves no current result.
3. Inspect supported task controls and verified environments. Do not invent environment IDs, capabilities, credentials, launches or delivered instructions.
4. Identify executing, completed, blocked and idle workers. Deduplicate accepted tasks, follow-ups, events and completed work.
5. Recompute the critical path, authorized ready queue, qualification readiness, capacity and next three work packages.
6. Dispatch dependency-ready authorized work; route completed candidates through applicable review, independent qualification and integration gates.
7. Record exact identities, stage, blocker owner, next action and observable timing in existing telemetry. Report material changes and decisions.

Use supported tools and authorized environments before requesting setup. A transient service error is not automatically an architectural blocker. Check whether a submission was accepted before any permitted retry; preserve the error and continue unaffected work. Respect access restrictions and report precise unresolved limitations.

Reassess scheduling at meaningful transitions and within an already configured supervisory reporting cycle. Deduplicate events and supplement monitoring with verified reads where needed. Do not imply unattended or scheduled execution unless a supported supervisor is actually configured. When no authorized work remains, prepare the next decision package rather than poll unproductively.

## Dependencies, safe parallelism and capacity

Maintain the project's existing dependency graph, predecessors/successors, blocking versus nonblocking edges, uncertain duration estimates and contribution to the next authorized milestone.

| Work | Scheduling |
|---|---|
| Source inspection, inventories, security analysis, documentation, contract crosswalks, independent fixture/oracle preparation and compatibility analysis. | Parallelize useful authorized disjoint work. |
| Components, independent batteries, adapters, typed-schema consumers and tooling. | Parallelize after applicable interface freeze and ownership separation. |
| Canonical contracts/state/admission, shared schemas, transactions, locks, migrations and integration. | One designated writer and serialized integration; independent analysis/tests may proceed concurrently. |
| External effects, upstream adoption, paid execution, real-environment resilience and deployment. | Require applicable compatibility and specific execution, financial and deployment authority. |

Prioritize critical-path mandatory blockers, independent test readiness and required security/environment prerequisites before optional work. Work-package numbering is not authority. When blocked, evaluate other useful authorized tasks; idleness is legitimate if none exist.

Keep a next-three-package view of earliest start, capabilities/environment, interface and independent-test readiness, expected duration/uncertainty and potential benefit. Reassess capacity after authorization/completion, new ready work, worker loss/blockage, qualification backlog or critical-path changes.

Add workers only for concrete independent work whose delay matters and expected benefit exceeds setup/coordination cost. Check existing capacity first, preserve active work where possible, and do not launch duplicates without an explicit review or comparative purpose. Estimate calendar benefit with uncertainty; worker count is not an objective.

Provision through supported tools within existing authority. If operator action is needed, provide one grouped request: role/repository, task and authority, bottleneck, benefit/uncertainty, independence, exact environment/setup, minimum permissions, cost/security implications and setup/prepare/defer recommendation. Never request credentials in chat or expand permissions or paid resources merely to add workers. Environment readiness and task authorization are separate.

## Independent qualification

Reuse existing harnesses, custody and reporting. The reusable flow is **Test specification → Configuration → Frozen invocation → Observation → Independent oracle → Evidence record**. Do not rebuild working components just to match terminology.

- **Specification:** versioned case, contract locator, fixture, expected outcome, preconditions, profile and evidence requirements.
- **Configuration:** exact implementation identity, module versions, requested versus effective enabled state, authority/policy versions and environment.
- **Invocation:** the actual supported interface and separately authorized execution profile.
- **Observation:** applicable outputs, causes, state before/after, negative evidence, version bindings, recovery, receipts and resource metrics.
- **Oracle:** independently governed expected bytes/hashes, predicates, transitions, negative controls and quality criteria.
- **Evidence:** source/protocol/fixture/configuration identities, environment, commands, timing, raw outputs/failures, checksums and reviewer/verdict provenance.

Prepare expectations, fixtures, harness, profile and exact handoff before acceptance. Aim for oracle readiness ahead of implementation where feasible, claiming that readiness only when verified. Freeze expectations before qualification execution, bind the exact subject and execute only authorized profiles. Keep proposed tests, executable tests, developer validation, review, execution and accepted verdict distinct.

Material changes to an oracle, fixture, threshold or evidence rule are Tier 3. Use one focused independent verifier as needed. Never change expected outcomes to make a candidate pass; correct genuine specification defects through sourced successors while preserving originals and escalating material reserved acceptance-rule changes.

Qualification covers applicable standalone contracts, refusal/failure, disabled/unavailable behavior, configuration eligibility, constitutional regressions, dependencies/interactions, provenance and functional and economic/latency claims. Use risk-based interactions rather than uncontrolled exhaustive combinations. A standalone pass does not establish interaction success; local/offline results do not establish real-environment or externally integrated qualification. Invalid adversarial fixtures are not operational bypasses.

## Blockers, evidence and economics

Classify blockers by source, contract, architecture, security, implementation, oracle, test environment, cloud/identity, external compatibility, upstream vendor, resources/budget or operator approval. Record severity, affected critical-path work, owner, earliest need, next action and resolution evidence.

Resolve routine CI, packaging, links, approved dependency setup and nonconflicting integration issues autonomously. Do not invent policy to resolve architectural ambiguity. Consolidate material decisions into one packet with exact source, conflict/gap, supported interpretation, alternatives, recommendation, architectural and oracle implications and scoped impact/deferral. Distinguish source-resolved, engineering-choice, operator-decision and deferred items. Deferral needs an applicability boundary, not a waiver.

Once documents are consistent and choices explicit, advance the executable handoff. Further documentation is justified by new material source evidence, demonstrated inconsistency, necessary executable interfaces or blocking security/constitutional defects. Do not repeat planning to restate old questions; preserve legitimate findings under EP17's reopening rule.

Preserve raw failures, blocked cases and predecessor fixtures/freezes. Verify observation-to-raw-record links, pointer resolution, explicit derivations and checksums. Missing requirements remain visible; partial results must not conceal an observed integrity failure. Exercise applicable negative/fault controls independently.

Keep technical cautions explicit in handoffs: ambiguous commit acknowledgment is not proved durable custody; receipts and model proposals create no authority. Source inspection is not an executed test, developer tests and green CI are not independent qualification, and a merge is not release or deployment. Do not manufacture missing coverage or infer PASS from unavailable mandatory checks. Additional workers cannot resolve missing authority. A cheaper or faster fallback must first preserve mandatory governance, quality and reliability floors.

Instrument available token/resource usage, dated provider rates, computation, storage/cloud costs, retries/escalations, refusals/failures, latency and independent acceptance. Primary economics is total cost per independently accepted deliverable, subject to governance, quality and reliability floors. Separate measured expenditure from counterfactual estimates; unknown usage remains unknown, savings count once and one synthetic case supports no general savings claim. A proposed budget is not spending authority.

## Evidence-gated integration

Merge routine compliant changes within standing authority when all applicable gates hold:

1. Scope is authorized; no unauthorized files, dependencies, upstream adoption or drift.
2. Controlling architecture/interfaces are satisfied without silent interpretation changes.
3. The exact current head is verified and required CI succeeded at that identity.
4. Required developer regressions and independent qualification have compatible evidence.
5. Risk-tier-required review is complete, no blocking findings remain, and repository protections are satisfied.
6. Integration creates no new work-package, spending, external-effect, deployment or release authority.

Apply Tier 1 self-validation without independent agent review or post-merge audit; Tier 2 adds code review only for concrete risk; Tier 3 uses one focused verifier as needed. Do not fabricate approval from a comment or bypass repository rules. Recheck head, scope and evidence immediately before merging, with expected-head safeguards where supported. Changed heads require affected re-review/requalification. If native auto-merge is unavailable, use an authorized direct merge rather than assuming checks will merge it or changing settings by assumption.

Verify the merge identity and intended content, record evidence in telemetry and reconcile dependencies. Continue the next already authorized ready task or prepare its decision package. Escalate reserved architectural/security/budget/external-effect/deployment/release/upstream or material acceptance-rule decisions; keep integration, qualification, release and deployment states distinct.

## Telemetry and reporting

Use designated existing controllers/registers, not competing dashboards. Independent qualification retains its own record; consolidated status does not overwrite its findings. Track readiness separately from authority and implementation separately from independent verdict and integration. Completion requires the assignment's applicable gates, not merely code or developer tests.

Task records include identity/work package/repository/owner, exact source/head, scope/authority, prerequisites, input/output contracts, required battery, environmental/external dependencies, state/timestamps, blocker/owner/next action and delivery/evidence links.

Measure authorization-to-independent-qualified outcome, with FAIL/BLOCKED timing separately; engineering time to reviewable implementation; queue/readiness/debugging/validation/handoff/rework/review/blocked intervals; independently qualified throughput, first-pass rate, regressions and escaped defects; operator burden; and coordination/capacity delays. Use retained timestamps. Active worker-hours remain unknown unless measured; commit intervals are not labor. Concurrency, PR/test/token/line counts and unsupported completion percentages are not primary productivity.

Worker handoffs identify scope, exact source/final identity, changed capabilities/files, commands/results/environment/failures, raw evidence, independent verdict versus author validation, dependencies, elapsed time/rework, blockers and next action. Preserve source/observation/inference distinctions.

Report at milestone completion, material blocker/decision, configured interval or explicit request. Consolidate progress, critical path, parallel assignments, oracle/verdict readiness, quality, timing, required operator decisions and next authorized action. Avoid unchanged-result floods and unnecessary continuation requests. Targets are no conflicting canonical writers, unauthorized work or unsupported qualification claims, with visible blocker ownership and oracles ready before acceptance—not utilization-driven busywork.
