# Concise operational output templates

Templates are **not grants**. Discover real values; never dispatch bracketed placeholders or unverified SHA paths. See [constitutional-articles.md](constitutional-articles.md) for exact clause cells.

## A. Eight-field builder assignment

**Imperative objective:** Complete the narrow authorized deliverable at the verified head.

1. **Identity:** work package; repo; base SHA; role; exactly one primary archetype A/B/C/D and its meaning.
2. **Authority:** controlling decision/ref, allowed actions, budget and external effect status, merge/deploy status.
3. **Ownership:** designated writer; writable paths; read-only contracts/oracles; conflict-free parallel work.
4. **Inputs:** static project plan, engineering constitution's locally adopted exact ref, frozen contracts/schema, evidence and dependencies.
5. **Acceptance:** T0–T4 consequence rationale, falsifiable positives/negatives/boundaries, actual checked commands or discovery step, exact-head new/reused tests, independent gate, CI Scope Declaration.
6. **Delivery:** PR/branch, final exact head, evidence and failure links, one concise handoff.
7. **Boundaries:** prohibitions, stop/reopen/approval conditions, escalation owner.
8. **Governance:** adoption and companion revision statuses; for every required archetype CA, copy exact `Required behavior` and `Prohibited shortcut` from adopted table; after each include `Binding: [specific verified path/ref/decision/test]`. Include additional material clauses.

A source-unknown draft says NOT DISPATCHABLE and states precise blocker. For user-specific builder formatting, put complete packet in one code block and include the static project plan and constitution references.

## B. One-decision blocker packet

- **Missing fact / counterexample:** precisely observed issue and what remains unverified.
- **Source / owner / scope:** exact contract, head, affected component and competent decider.
- **Choices:** smallest legitimate alternatives, including defer and no-change.
- **Consequences:** authority/security/compatibility, oracle/evidence and cost/critical-path impact.
- **Decision requested:** one concrete bounded choice, no invented default.
- **Unaffected work:** authorized ready tasks that can continue.

## C. PR review and CI Scope Declaration

- Current exact head, baseline, changed paths and transitive callers; T0–T4 + impact rationale.
- Contract/requirements, affected EP/CA, external reliance and compatibility.
- New tests: commands, inputs, environments, observed PASS/FAIL, negatives/failure paths.
- Reused tests: exact prior source/config/contract/environment and documented applicability.
- Skips and NOT EXECUTED; source/evidence blockers, original adverse material and disposition.
- Independent oracle status, required reviewer/branch protection, holds and authorized spending/effects.
- Verdict: safe for *specific requested stage* / change needed / BLOCKED. Never imply certification or deployment from merge.

## D. Independent qualification handoff

- Protocol and oracle owner, version and frozen expected outcomes **before** observations.
- Exact subject/source/working tree, dependencies, policy/config/effective state, environment/time/inputs.
- Invocation/commands, raw traces/receipts, negative evidence, custody/checksums and recovery.
- Each case PASS/FAIL/BLOCKED/NOT ASSESSED/N/A with source-backed predicate.
- Comparison and scope limitations; independent assessor identity, qualification/verdict and remaining approval boundary.

## E. Amendment or adoption decision

- Proposer, predecessor revision exact identity, requested editorial/clarifying/normative delta, affected clauses.
- Inspection findings, negative evidence, alternatives, compatibility and retained obligations.
- Risk/required independent review, adoption scope/effective time/authority/reserved decisions.
- Accepted/rejected/deferred result, exact new revision and canonical lineage; local transition/exception if authorized.

## F. Supervisor milestone update

- Last verified source/head; completed and independently qualified stages **separate**; merged and deployed stages **separate**.
- Critical path, three next ready bounded tasks, owner, dependent contract and capacity/readiness.
- New material failures/holds, operator decision if necessary, observed timing, one next action.
- No unchanged-status flood, estimated worker hours passed off as measured, or claim of unconfigured monitoring.
