# Adoption, authority, amendments and custody

Source: `ADOPTION.md`; Constitution EP2–EP7, CA06, CA07, CA15, CA17, CA19–CA23.

## Adoption is a separate event

A constitutional publication, available copy, code merge, signed artifact, passing CI, implementation deployment or human presence does not itself establish organizational adoption or authority. Competent adopting authority must record (1) exact content/revision identity; (2) scope/project and effective point; (3) overriding law/security/contract and local rules; (4) explicitly reserved powers; (5) adopted addenda, exceptions/transition provisions; (6) accountable decision and lineage. Old frozen projects stay governed by their selected revision until explicit lawful transition.

## Functional roles

- **Proposer:** suggests amendment, no adoption authority by that fact.
- **Inspector:** checks consistency, evidence, regression, alternatives; inspection is not certification.
- **Independent qualifier:** owns independent obligations/oracle and scoped evidence; no automatic independence from second automated process.
- **Adopting authority:** competent identified individual/body decides specified text/scope/effective point. Mere human approval without competence is insufficient.
- **Integrator/custodian:** records/publishes canonical accepted material within delegated decision; does not expand approval.

Same person may perform several roles only if governing risk and independence allow. A delegated agent can perform bounded authorized work, not enlarge or silently transfer scope.

## Seven-step amendment procedure

1. **Propose** exact predecessor and impacted EP/CA, material semantic change, rationale/evidence/alternatives.
2. **Inspect** authority, constitution coherence, reliance boundaries, evidence lineage, feasibility, backward compatibility and negatives.
3. **Review proportionately**, using highest relevant tier and focused independent review for changes to authority, certification, mandatory floor or history semantics.
4. **Decide** with competent authority (accept/reject/defer) specifying exact wording and scope; merge cannot substitute.
5. **Canonicalize** new distinct revision with ancestry, effective conditions, accountable acceptance and change record; preserve predecessor and material failures.
6. **Adopt locally** for each project through explicit selection and transitions, not silent global update.
7. **Correct or supersede** through controlled reopening for material defects/new evidence/contradictions/competent instruction; preserve original lineage and explain successor.

Classify revisions as **editorial**, **clarifying**, **normative**; do not mislabel behavior change editorial to evade review. Record stable revision/version and exact content SHA/hash. Normative changes specify changed obligations/permitted behavior, affected dependencies, compatibility and needed renegotiation. An incompatible external commitment needs an authorized migration or explicit lawful exception; do not claim transparent backward compatibility unsupported by evidence.

## Evidence and exception conditions

Retain accepted/rejected/old source text and dated decisions, material dissent, objections, adverse results and supersession, subject to lawful privacy/access/retention. A newer policy does not retroactively certify prior behavior. A waiver needs actually competent authority, exact scope/duration, reason, controls and retained evidence. No template or proposed addendum grants spending, credential access, employment delegation, runtime dispatch, merge, release, certification, production effect or access to secrets.

## Disputed or absent sources

If project adoption, authority, text, rule applicability or environmental evidence is unknown, distinguish directly observed and source-backed facts from inference. Retrieve exact source; if still unresolved record `UNVERIFIED` and stop impacted action, continue unaffected permitted work. A model proposal, plausible summary or previous conversational statement is not a substitute for the controlling primary source.
