# Cognous Engineering Constitution

A secular, static, technology-neutral framework for governed engineering. It distinguishes **the right to propose a change** from **the authority to make it canonical** and protects provenance, justified reliance, independent assessment and accountable evolution.

- [Engineering Constitution](ENGINEERING_CONSTITUTION.md) — seven engineering principles (EP1–EP7), 23 linked constitutional articles (CA01–CA23), risk tiers and evidence discipline.
- [Risk-tiered testing policy](TESTING_POLICY.md) — minimum sufficient evidence, change-risk tiers T0–T4, safe CI scope selection, and independent qualification boundaries.
- [Task-scoped engineering compliance addendum](ENGINEERING_COMPLIANCE_ADDENDUM.md) — fixed role/archetype routing, verbatim constitutional article slices and source-backed assignment bindings.
- [Engineering orchestration](ORCHESTRATION.md) — static practice for coordinating authorized work without acquiring authority over independent judgment.
- [Adoption and amendment](ADOPTION.md) — version selection, competent adoption, amendment lineage, scope and compatibility.

These texts state enduring guidance; they do not constitute an implementation plan, active task register, status dashboard or authorization for activity. Specific systems choose their own implementations and may only claim adoption of a particular revision through an authorized, recorded act.

Technical acceptance, independent qualification, canonical integration, institutional adoption and deployment are separate events. A merge, signature, automated verdict or publication does not collapse those distinctions.

The documents are intentionally free of named implementation projects, product architectures and source-repository references. General examples never make a particular tool or platform mandatory.
